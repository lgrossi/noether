Noether · wireframe exploration · v1

What does Noether want to be?

The product story is strong (local governance for agent work — observe, attribute, control, simulate) but the current UI tries to be a dashboard of everything. Below are five distinct identities Noether could commit to. Each picks a different center of gravity, a different first verb, a different thing that gets killed. Pick one — or mix.

↑ this is the killer feature competitors don't have

why this worksIt leads with what's unique. LiteLLM can't simulate. Datadog can't simulate. Stripe can't simulate. A user who lands here learns "this thing is for trying policies, not for staring at numbers."
what you give upDay-to-day operational awareness. Someone who just wants to know "did anything weird happen today" has to click to find out. The lab as homepage assumes you're thinking, not watching.
watch out forIf your scenarios feel like demos rather than real teams' shapes, the whole thing feels like marketing. Replay-against-real-data is the credibility move.

why this worksIt refuses to be a dashboard. You "talk" to your governance layer the way you talk to git or jq. Discoverability lives in suggested follow-ups.
what you give upVisual overview. People who think in tables and charts get nothing on first load. Best when wrapped with a fallback (the right-side mock) for the non-CLI half of users.
watch out forNatural-language answers can lie if they wrap real data carelessly. Every reply must show the underlying query/numbers so it's auditable — same posture Noether already takes about decisions.
